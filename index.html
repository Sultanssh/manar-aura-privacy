English

No internet

The app has no permission to use the internet, so it neither sends nor receives any data.

No tracking

The app has no accounts, no ads, and no analytics or tracking of any kind.

Your files

The app reaches only the folder you choose yourself (Honor/Themes) through Android, and the pictures and fonts you pick by hand; nothing else.

Storage

Settings, saved colours and the log of operations stay on your device only, and are removed when the app is uninstalled.

Google colours

This feature needs an optional permission that can only be granted by hand with adb, and is used for nothing else.

Independence

Manar Aura is an independent app by one person; it is not affiliated with or endorsed by Honor.
